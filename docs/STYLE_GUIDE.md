# Austenpunk Book Cover Style Guide

## Purpose

This document defines the visual identity of **Austenpunk Publishing**
book covers. Every cover should look like a genuine late-Victorian
technical handbook from an alternate universe where classic authors
wrote software engineering books.

## Core Visual Concept

-   Victorian technical manual rather than fantasy art.
-   Engraved literary illustration.
-   Dry, intelligent, affectionate satire.
-   Software concepts expressed through nineteenth-century imagery.

## House Style Invariants

-   Portrait 2:3 aspect ratio.
-   Cream/parchment background.
-   Ornate engraved border.
-   Publisher plaque: **AUSTENPUNK PUBLISHING**.
-   Large engraved serif title.
-   Smaller subtitle.
-   Large central illustration.
-   Author plaque at bottom.
-   Warm sepia palette.

## Layout

Top: publisher plaque.

Upper third: title and subtitle.

Centre: engraved illustration.

Lower section: contents panel and quotation panel.

Bottom: author plaque.

## Typography

-   Victorian engraved serif fonts.
-   Large title.
-   Smaller centred subtitle.
-   Author in capitals.
-   Break long titles over multiple lines for readability.

## Colour & Texture

Use: - cream - ivory - parchment - sepia - muted browns - navy -
charcoal

Include: - aged paper - engraved ink - subtle foxing - cloth-bound feel

Avoid black horror-poster backgrounds.

## Illustration Style

Use Victorian engravings and book illustrations.

Aim for: - 70%% literary scene - 30%% software metaphor

Represent software concepts with: - blueprints - ledgers - filing
cabinets - diagrams - maps - engineering drawings

Avoid modern laptops, phones and UI overlays.

## Prompt Template

``` text
Create a portrait Victorian book cover for Austenpunk Publishing.

Title: {title}
Subtitle: {subtitle}
Author: {author}
Topic: {topic}

Use a warm cream parchment background.

Include:
- ornate engraved border
- publisher plaque reading "AUSTENPUNK PUBLISHING"
- large engraved serif title
- smaller subtitle
- author plaque at bottom
- Victorian illustration visualising:

{teaser}

Supporting ideas:

{blurb}

Include a small Contents panel and one quotation panel.

Overall style:
Victorian technical handbook.
Elegant.
Literary.
Humorous.
Believable.
```

## Negative Prompt

Avoid: - horror poster aesthetic - black backgrounds - glossy AI art -
cyberpunk - stock photography - modern offices - fake typography -
unreadable text - incorrect aspect ratio

Preserve exactly: - title - subtitle - author - publisher

## Representative Covers

-   Dracula and Access Control
-   Howards End-to-End Testing
-   Sherlock Holmes and the Case of the Missing Semicolon
-   The Sign of Four Oh Four
-   Great Expectations Management

Early versions of: - The Hound of the Baskervilles and Incident
Response - Jude the Obscure Legacy System - The Picture of Dorian
Graylog

were too dark and should be treated as examples of what to avoid.

## Production Notes

Recommended size: **1024×1536**.

Generate the complete cover including title, subtitle, publisher and
author.

Review each cover to ensure: - correct text - warm palette - Victorian
engraving style - immediate visual joke - consistency with the existing
catalogue.

## Philosophy

Every cover should make the reader think:

> This looks absurdly plausible.

The ideal Austenpunk cover feels like a forgotten Victorian technical
handbook discovered in an antiquarian bookshop.
